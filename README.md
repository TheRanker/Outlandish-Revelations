# Outlandish Revelations

*A Wythers Wonderworld*

**Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge · Tech, magic, and exploration**

[Download on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/outlandish-revelations)

Start with a small Create workshop. Build it into a GregTech factory, then take what you’ve made out into a world of sprawling biomes, old ruins, and people waiting for help. **Outlandish Revelations** ties custom recipes and industrial progression to an adventure that spans four dimensions.

![Features](https://bisecthosting.com/images/CF/Outlandish_Revelations/BH_OR_features.webp)

## Terrain and biomes

Large Biomes give each region room to breathe. Follow rivers through broad valleys, cross dense woodland, and look for a place with room for a workshop and a rail line. The Overworld is shaped by [William Wythers’ Overhauled Overworld](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/william-wythers-overhauled-overworld) and [William Wythers’ Expanded Ecosphere](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/expanded-ecosphere).

Beyond the Overworld, explore Incendium’s Nether, Nullscape’s outer End islands, and the Twilight Forest.

## Architecture

Build a water-powered mill on a riverbank, run rail through the hills, or grow a MineColonies settlement around your factory. Along the way you’ll find homes, forges, and workshops with their own stories. Bring the right supplies and you can help restore them, one repair at a time.

## GregTech and Create

Use Create for your first production lines, move into Steam, and work your way up GregTech’s voltage tiers. Custom recipes connect machinery, ore processing, storage, tools, and magic across the pack. As your workshop grows, use Applied Energistics 2 to organize production and Tinkers’ Construct to make tools for the next expedition.

## The Unfinished Promise

The Ranker learned to capture the powers of creatures and bind them into relics. His pursuit of the Hovering Inferno left twelve people waiting for help he had promised.

Find them across the Overworld, Nether, outer End, and Twilight Forest. Carry the **Scroll of the Unfinished Promise**, hear their stories, and help rebuild what they lost. Their accounts reveal the history of the sword **Tears for Fears** and the **Ring of Ranker**, and lead to a memorial where you can awaken both relics.

![Overview](https://bisecthosting.com/images/CF/Outlandish_Revelations/BH_OR_overview.webp)

## Questbook and delivery journal

The illustrated **FTB questbook** guides you through industry, magic, and all twelve NPC stories. The **Outlandish Player Handbook** gives you a reference for ore processing, workshop progression, and relic controls.

Hold your scroll and **sneak + right-click in the air** to open **My Deliveries**. Check what each NPC still needs, see where you last spoke, and set an FTB Chunks waypoint for the trip back.

## Featured mods

- **Industry and storage** — GregTech CEu Modern, Create, Applied Energistics 2, Ender IO, Integrated Dynamics, and Sophisticated Backpacks
- **Magic and equipment** — Blood Magic, Botania, Apotheosis, Forbidden and Arcanus, Tinkers’ Construct, and Draconic Evolution
- **World and adventure** — William Wythers’ world generation, Incendium, Nullscape, Twilight Forest, End Remastered, Progressive Bosses, Realm RPG, and YUNG’s structure mods
- **Building and everyday play** — MineColonies, SecurityCraft, Farmer’s Delight, and Pam’s HarvestCraft 2
- **Quests and navigation** — FTB Quests, FTB Chunks, and Quest Giver 2

This repository contains the pack’s modified profile files. The playable modpack is distributed through CurseForge.
